## Build 1012: A calmer top bar: three shapes, one menu and the views in the centre

2026-11-10

Every screen now has a new top bar made of three separate shapes instead of one strip: the app's name on the left, the views in the middle, and the menu on the right. A merchandiser sees Board, Review (with the number waiting) and Setup in the middle, and the one you are on is underlined; a supervisor sees the page title there, so both roles get the same look. The middle stays exactly centred on the screen whatever the sides hold. The menu opens and moves with the keyboard (arrow keys, Home, End, Escape) and keeps every item it had, including the dark and light choice and the language. On a phone a merchandiser's views become a row that scrolls sideways under the bar, and Tamil titles and 200% zoom fit without anything running off the screen. The bar slides in once when the app opens, not again on every page, and not at all if the phone is set to reduce motion. The status page and the buyer page show the app's name only; the sign-in page has no bar.

## Build 1009: Safer and cheaper to run: a bug round on the backend, faster updates, and photos without location data

2026-11-08

After every update the server now recomputes only the order that changed instead of every order, so sending an update and opening the board are much quicker and cheaper to run. A long audit found and fixed dozens of small bugs: a stage exactly on pace could show red, a warning could be emailed to a buyer twice, a mistyped date far in the future could freeze the server, a blank purge value could delete every rejected photo, and sign-in revealed which phone numbers have an account. Photos are stripped of camera and location data before they are stored, two people changing the same ship date at once no longer garble its history, and a wrong storage address is refused at start-up. Pages that people reload every minute are now compressed and sent only when they changed.

## Build 1005: Say why a stage is slow: a reason and a note, in Tamil or English

## Build 1004: A photo with the daily update, and a steadier daily rate

2026-11-03

A supervisor can add a photo to a daily update. The phone shrinks it first (a large camera picture becomes roughly 150 KB) and sends it with the update, even after a lost signal or a lost reply, without ever making a duplicate. A merchandiser approves or rejects it in the review queue, and a buyer sees only approved photos of their own orders. Separately, the daily rate is now worked out from completed days, so a small update sent in the morning no longer drags a late-risk order's rate down and raises a false warning.
