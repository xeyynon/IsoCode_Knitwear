# Changelog

Generated by `scripts/changelog.mjs`. Do not edit by hand. Newest first.

## Build 1005: Say why a stage is slow: a reason and a note, in Tamil or English

2026-11-03

A supervisor can now add to a daily update a reason (one tap from a short list: not enough workers, workers on leave, machine problem, material late, no power, fixing quality, something else) and a few typed words in Tamil or English. They travel with the update, even with no signal, and arrive exactly once. A merchandiser reviews each note in the review queue, adds the English meaning of a Tamil note and checks the reason; notes also appear on the order's page. Notes are internal and never shown to buyers. An update can now only attach a photo that really belongs to that order.

## Build 1004: A photo with the daily update, and a steadier daily rate

2026-11-03

A supervisor can add a photo to a daily update. The phone shrinks it first (a large camera picture becomes roughly 150 KB) and sends it with the update, even after a lost signal or a lost reply, without ever making a duplicate. A merchandiser approves or rejects it in the review queue, and a buyer sees only approved photos of their own orders. Separately, the daily rate is now worked out from completed days, so a small update sent in the morning no longer drags a late-risk order's rate down and raises a false warning.

## Build 1003: Nothing reaches a buyer until you confirm it, and a buyer sees only their own orders

2026-11-03

Warnings about at-risk stages now wait in a review queue. A merchandiser reads the wording a buyer would see (kept free of internal figures), can reword it, then confirms it or hides it; changing a stage's colour needs a fresh look. Merchandisers can make a private link for one buyer contact, set how long it works, and cancel it. The buyer opens it with no sign-in and sees only their own orders, planned dates and confirmed warnings, read-only. A link opens nothing else, and a missing, unknown, cancelled or expired link all give the same message.

## Build 1002: Set up an order and see exactly why it has its colour

2026-11-03

Merchandisers can create an order from a form and see, before creating it, the date each stage must finish, worked backwards from the ex-factory date. Opening an order from the board shows each stage with the planned finish date beside the date it will really finish at the current rate, how far apart they are, the progress and the working behind the colour. The ex-factory date can be changed there; a reason is required and every change is kept in a history showing who, when, what and why.

## Build 1001: Supervisor daily update that works with no signal

2026-11-03

Supervisors now see their own orders and stages in My orders today, tap one, type the pieces made and send. The update is saved on the phone first and sent when there is signal, with a chip showing All sent or how many are waiting. The app itself opens with no signal and shows the last saved list. Updates survive closing and reopening the app, a failed send can always be retried safely without counting twice, and an update the server refuses is kept with the reason instead of vanishing. Warnings appear if the phone cannot keep updates safely or storage is almost full.

## Build 1000: Sign in and the order board

2026-11-03

Merchandisers can sign in and see every order on one board, worst first, with the working behind each colour (for example: need 467 a day, making 350 a day, 700 pieces short by 11 Nov). Filter by status or buyer; the filters stay in the address. Supervisors can sign in with a phone number and PIN, and the Tamil status words are shown. A public status page shows whether the system is working.
