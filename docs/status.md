A clash with the supervisor's tap is called out. The merchandiser decides; their reason is stored apart from the AI's. Buyers never see notes. No key: the queue says AI is off (`noteAi.ts`, `noteAiGroq.ts`, `noteAiGemini.ts`, `aiFromEnv.ts`, `review/noteAiLogic.ts`) | Built, tested with a stubbed model; checked live on the demo |
